Privacy policy · 6 September 2026

Privacy

Your policy fixtures stay on your device. The sample runs in your browser and the CLI reads files you name.

What this product collects

Proxy Policy Rehearsal has no accounts, analytics, trackers, cookies, or telemetry. The hosted documentation can create ordinary server access logs for security and reliability.

Your policy fixtures

The CLI reads files you choose. It does not query live DNS, call IP-intelligence feeds, or upload policy data. The browser sample makes no third-party requests.

Demo storage

The sample uses the browser key demo:ppr:fixture while the Demo banner is shown. Reset demo restores the bundled fixture. Start for real removes that demo key. The demo never reads or writes real product data.

Third parties

No third-party fonts, scripts, ad services, payment processors, or IP-intelligence feeds run on this site. The source link opens GitHub only when you choose it.

Contact

For a privacy question, open an issue in the project’s public source repository. Do not include sensitive policy or production addresses.
