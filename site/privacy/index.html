Privacy

Your rehearsal inputs stay yours. The CLI is offline and the browser demo processes text only in your current tab.

What we collect

Proxy Policy Rehearsal does not include analytics, trackers, accounts, cookies, or telemetry. The hosted documentation may produce ordinary server access logs managed by the hosting operator, such as time, requested path, and network address, for security and reliability.

Your policy data

The CLI reads only files you name and makes no network requests. The browser demo does not upload, persist, or place its editor content in local storage. Closing or reloading the page discards edits.

Third parties

No third-party fonts, scripts, ad services, payment processors, or IP-intelligence feeds run on this site.

Contact

For a privacy question, open an issue in the project’s public source repository. Do not include sensitive policy or production addresses.
